# Solace library music

Solace's background music: fifteen calm classical recordings for studying, 80 minutes in all, every one
**public domain or CC0** (no CC BY-SA, no NC). **New World ▸ Build Solace Library** plays them in the order below
(`SolaceOrder` in `Editor/SolaceBuilder.cs`), with any other recordings added here after them, streamed and looping
the playlist. If this folder is empty, Solace falls back to `Solace/Audio`, then to three calm tracks from the café,
the lobby and the meadow. (`Solace/Composers/*/Audio` hold the plaques' short Listen excerpts and are not used here.)

The order opens calmly with the Goldberg Aria and never plays the same composer twice in a row. It ends with the
Aria da capo, so the playlist loops back round to the Aria, as the Goldberg Variations themselves do.

## Rights

- **CC0**: Kimiko Ishizaka's *Open Goldberg Variations* was released under CC0 (tracks 1 and 15). Musopen's
  *Complete Chopin Collection* on the Internet Archive is CC0 1.0 (track 9).
- **Public domain (Musopen)**: Musopen (musopen.org, a non-profit) releases its recordings into the public domain.
  The Commons pages tag them `PD-author` (Musopen), most with Wikimedia permission ticket 2008012110017088. The
  Kickstarter recordings (tracks 2, 6, 10 and 12) are also in https://archive.org/details/MusopenCollectionAsFlac
  under the Public Domain Mark. As a courtesy, though not as a legal condition, Musopen asks for credit and asks that
  its recordings are not sold on their own. The app credits Musopen on every one of its tracks and does not sell the
  recordings separately.
- Left out on purpose: the CC BY-SA recordings on Commons (for example eldüendesüarez's Debussy, Beethoven and Brahms,
  Michael Laucke's Satie on guitar), Kimiko Ishizaka's Well-Tempered Clavier files (tagged CC BY 3.0 on Commons) and
  the historic 78 rpm transfers (their public-domain status differs from country to country). No clean public-domain or
  CC0 Debussy or Liszt recording was found, so they are not in the playlist. The garden's tracks (Chopin's Op. 9, 27/2,
  48/1 and C-sharp minor nocturnes, Rachmaninoff) are not repeated here.

## Processing

Each source (MP3, FLAC or Ogg from Commons) was resampled to 44.1 kHz, with silence trimmed from both ends and a
2-second fade in and out. Solo piano was mixed down to mono; the quartets and the concerto stay stereo. A plain gain
(no compression) brought each one to -20 LUFS integrated, with a look-ahead limiter at -1.5 dBTP for the rare peak.
They were then encoded as Ogg Vorbis q4 with ffmpeg: 41.6 MiB in all. Unity imports them as streamed Vorbis at
quality 0.5, close to the source, so they don't take up more space in the build.

## Credits

`CREDITS.txt` holds one line per recording: the file name without its extension, a bar, then the credit, e.g.

    Bach - Goldberg Variations - Aria | Kimiko Ishizaka · Open Goldberg · CC0

Solace shows the credit small under the track name in its menu and on the desk's music panel. The **i** beside
MUSIC in the menu, and the one on the desk panel, open the **Music credits** card, which lists every track. Rebuilding
Solace warns about any recording in this folder that has no line in `CREDITS.txt`.
